# scicomm 0.1.0

* First release.
* Structured description of a scientific finding and of the texts that communicate it: `sc_finding()`, `sc_version()`, `sc_example()`.
* Fidelity of each text to the finding: causal language, population, non-human studies, direction, numbers, magnitude, certainty and limitations: `sc_fidelity()`.
* Clarity, accessibility and uncertainty indicators in English and Spanish, including the Flesch reading ease and the Szigriszt-Pazos perspicuity index: `sc_clarity()`, `sc_accessibility()`, `sc_uncertainty()`, `sc_readability()`.
* Comparison across audiences and a self-contained HTML report: `sc_assess()`, `sc_compare()`, `sc_report()`.
* Configurable targets and documented lexicons: `sc_targets()`, `sc_lexicon()`.
